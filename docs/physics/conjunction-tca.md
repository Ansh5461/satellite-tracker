# Conjunction TCA screening

**Stage 1:** apogee/perigee band overlap.  
**Stage 2:** vectorized pairwise \(\|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j\|\) on a coarse grid.  
**Stage 3:** root of \(\dot\rho=\mathbf r_\mathrm{rel}\cdot\mathbf v_\mathrm{rel}=0\) via Brent; report miss distance.

**References:** Hoots, Schumacher & Glover, *History of Analytical Orbit Modeling in the U.S. Space Surveillance System*, 2004.
