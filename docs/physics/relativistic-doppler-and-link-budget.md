# Relativistic Doppler and FSPL

Classical downlink Doppler:

\[
\Delta f = -f_0\frac{\dot\rho}{c}
\]

Special-relativistic form (source moving, receiver ≈ rest):

\[
f_r = \frac{f_0}{\gamma\bigl(1+\dot\rho/c\bigr)},\qquad
\gamma=\bigl(1-v^2/c^2\bigr)^{-1/2}
\]

Transverse term at L-band LEO is \(\sim 0.5\,\mathrm{Hz}\). Drift:

\[
\dot f \approx -f_0\frac{\ddot\rho}{c}
\]

Free-space path loss (ITU-R P.525):

\[
\mathrm{FSPL[dB]} = 20\log_{10}\bigl(4\pi\,d\,f/c\bigr)
\]

**References:** ITU-R P.525; Vallado; special relativity texts.
